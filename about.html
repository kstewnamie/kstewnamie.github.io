About the Artist

Making colour feel like home

"I paint the way I feel; each canvas loud, warm, and with as much colour as possible."

Based in beautiful Norfolk County, Ontario, I paint bold acrylics on canvas of birds, animals, and everyday moments. I aim for my artwork to always be expressive and always a little bit wild.

Whether you're looking for a statement piece for your living room or a small painting to brighten up a shelf, you're in the right place.
