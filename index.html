About the Artist

Making colour feel like home

"I paint the way I feel — loudly, warmly, and with as much colour as possible."

Based in beautiful Norfolk County, Ontario, I've been creating art for over two decades. My work spans bold acrylic paintings on canvas to playful digital illustrations — always expressive, always a little bit wild.

Whether you're looking for a statement piece for your living room, a print to brighten up your office, or a sticker that just makes you smile — you're in the right place.
